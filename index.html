Settings
Appearance
Background photoNEW

Your photo becomes the app background. It is saved on this device and, when you are logged in, with your account together with your profile photo.

LanguageNEW

Names, search and the chat bot accept letters from any language. Menu text is translated for the main screens; the rest stays in English.

Passkey (Face ID / fingerprint)NEW

Uses this device's Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint through WebAuthn. Your face or fingerprint never leaves the device. The passkey works only on this device and only on a secure (https) address.

Account & Cloud sync

Log in with your username and PIN and your history, theme, language and profile photo are saved to that account, encrypted on the server. Log in with the same username and PIN on any device to get them back. Logging out removes them from this device.
